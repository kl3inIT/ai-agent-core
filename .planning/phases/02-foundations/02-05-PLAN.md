## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-02-CM-02 | Denial of Service | Dual DDL (Liquibase + Spring AI auto-init) creates conflict, boot fails | mitigate | `initialize-schema=never` disables Spring AI side; single source of truth is our changelog |
| T-02-CM-03 | Information Disclosure | SPRING_AI_CHAT_MEMORY rows cross user boundaries (no row-level policy on raw table) | accept | Table is Spring-AI-owned internal memory; Phase 4 `ChatService` enforces conversationId ownership at boundary; raw table isn't exposed to DataManager queries |
