# Fixture Beta — Beta Product Pricing Tiers

This deterministic fixture is used by the Phase 5 RAG integration tests to verify
role-scoped retrieval. It is synthetic sales content (Beta Product is not a real
product) and contains no customer data.

## Starter Tier

The Beta Product Starter tier is designed for individual developers and small teams
that want to evaluate the platform. The tier is priced at a flat monthly rate per
seat and includes the full core feature set with a generous usage allowance. Support
is provided through the community forum and a self-service knowledge base.

## Growth Tier

The Growth tier targets scaling teams that need higher usage limits, shared
workspaces, and business-hours email support. Pricing is tier-based by committed
seats and includes a sixty day contractual renewal window. Administrators can add
overage capacity on demand for predictable month-end billing.

## Enterprise Tier

The Enterprise tier includes dedicated success management, single sign-on, audit
log export, and custom volume pricing. Customers on the Enterprise tier receive a
quarterly business review and access to a dedicated support engineer for high
priority incidents. Contracts are typically annual with negotiated service level
commitments.

## Discounts

Annual prepayment receives a fifteen percent discount across all tiers. Education,
non-profit, and open-source organisations may apply for additional targeted
discounts through the Beta Product partnership programme.
