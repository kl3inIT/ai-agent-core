# Fixture Gamma — Gamma Internal Compliance Policy

This deterministic fixture is used by the Phase 5 RAG integration tests to verify
admin-only document visibility. It is synthetic content (Gamma is not a real
company) and contains no privileged or regulated information.

## Scope

This compliance policy covers data classification, access control, vendor due
diligence, and incident response for all information systems operated by Gamma. It
applies to every employee and contractor with production system access, regardless
of role, seniority, or duration of engagement.

## Data Classification

Gamma data is classified into four tiers — Public, Internal, Confidential, and
Restricted. Restricted data includes authentication secrets, personally
identifiable information, payment card data, and material non-public business
information. Access to Restricted data requires documented business justification
and quarterly access review.

## Vendor Due Diligence

Every vendor that processes Gamma data must complete a security questionnaire and
provide evidence of current independent security certification. Vendor agreements
must include a data processing addendum with breach notification clauses tied to
the applicable regulatory framework. The security team maintains an approved vendor
list and reviews new onboardings monthly.

## Incident Response

Security incidents are reported to the security team through the internal incident
channel. The on-call responder triages within fifteen minutes of receipt and
coordinates containment, eradication, and recovery according to the published
runbook. Executive leadership is notified for any incident classified as high or
critical severity.
